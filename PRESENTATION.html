  • Models do not contain every organization’s current, private, or regulated evidence.
  • Retrieval selects what enters the reasoning context.
  • Poor retrieval creates confident conclusions from missing or incorrectly grouped evidence.
  • Drug research depends on exact product, strength, route, form, and regulatory status.
If retrieval is wrong, better generation cannot rescue the answer.
